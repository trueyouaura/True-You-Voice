YOUR PRACTICE MAP

Explore one thing at a time.

  1. Ease first

    Unclench your jaw, let shoulders settle, and breathe normally.

  2. Resonance & listening

    Compare comfortable sounds at a similar pitch. Notice the difference in tone, not a number.

  3. Everyday speech

    Bring one easy change into a short sentence. Your voice should remain sustainable.

Resonance is the filtering of sound by your vocal tract. This app does not measure formants or rate resonance, gender, or “passing.” A forward vibration is an optional sensation cue, not a measurement.

YOUR VOICE DESERVES CARE

Keep it comfortable. Keep it yours.

Safety comes before a target.

  • Use an easy conversational volume. Never squeeze your throat, force your larynx upward, or chase a number through discomfort.
  • Take frequent rests and drink water. Short sessions are enough; stop when tired.
  • Stop immediately for pain, burning, tightness, or increasing hoarseness. Avoid practice when your voice is hoarse or you are ill. Don’t whisper to push through fatigue.
  • If discomfort or voice changes persist, or speaking becomes difficult, consult a qualified speech-language pathologist or clinician. Breathing difficulty needs urgent medical attention.

This is an educational practice tool, not diagnosis or individualized treatment. There is no single feminine voice or pitch range, and the app cannot guarantee how anyone will perceive you.

Private by design.

Microphone audio and pitch analysis stay in your browser. No analytics, audio uploads, or external fonts. The web host still receives normal page requests.

Microphone access starts only when you connect it. Disconnecting, switching away from the page, or closing the page stops capture. Switching away also pauses practice.

Profiles, session notes, and settings use local browser storage. Each profile has separate practice data; nothing is sent to an account service. Saved recordings use IndexedDB and stay local. Other people using this browser profile may access them. Private browsing, clearing browser data, or changing device or site address may remove or separate your data.

Download clips and export progress to keep your own backups. Deleting data here cannot delete files you already downloaded.

Appearance & color vision

Blue & Gold, Teal & Rose, Monochrome, and High Contrast emphasize readable text and controls. The chart uses a solid pitch line, dashed range boundaries, and numeric summaries in every theme. Monochrome removes hue differences; High Contrast offers black-on-white or white-on-black. Choose whichever is easiest for your vision and lighting. Simulations and contrast checks are not a guarantee for every person, especially when light sensitivity or reduced acuity is also present.

Microphone troubleshooting

Use HTTPS or localhost. Open the app directly, allow microphone access in your browser’s site settings, and check your system’s microphone permission. Close other apps using the microphone. Headphones help keep playback out of the tracker.

Quiet rooms give better estimates. Unvoiced consonants, background music, breathiness, and vocal fry can leave gaps or produce octave errors. The tracker estimates F0 between 65–650 Hz, not a clinical measurement.
